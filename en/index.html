About Emil's

From Sandviken to all of Bergen

Emil's Kebab & Pizzeria is located at Støletorget in Sandviken and has become a regular stop for many in central Bergen. We roll the kebabs, stretch the pizza dough and shape the burgers ourselves, with fresh ingredients every day.

That effort is what Bergens Tidende noticed when the newspaper named us the city's best kebab, and what Wolt recognised with the Kebab of the Year award in 2023.
